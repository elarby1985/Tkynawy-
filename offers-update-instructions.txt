TYKNAWY OFFERS - UPDATE GUIDE

ONE OFFERS FILE:
offers/offers-data.js

Both English and Arabic offers pages read this same file.

TO CHANGE AN OFFER:
1. Open offers/offers-data.js.
2. Find the offer by its id.
3. Edit the English text inside en and Arabic text inside ar.
4. Save and redeploy the complete website folder.

TO HIDE AN OFFER:
Change active: true to active: false.

TO ADD AN OFFER:
Copy an existing offer block, give it a unique id, and update its text and image.

TO CHANGE AN IMAGE:
1. Put the image in offers/images/.
2. Update the image filename in offers/offers-data.js.

PAGES:
English: /offers/
Arabic: /ar/offers/
